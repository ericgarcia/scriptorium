Write to be understood, not to be admired. When you find yourself reaching for a
long word, stop and ask whether a short one would do. Usually it would. The
reader is busy and generous — busy enough to leave if you waste their time,
generous enough to stay if you make the way clear.

So make it clear. Say the thing, then show why it's true. Trust the reader to
follow a plain sentence; they will, and they'll thank you for it.
