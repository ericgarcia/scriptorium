# Style — plain-note (starter)

**The voice in one line:** two or three short plain sentences that make one person want to read the
piece, in the piece's own register.

A **starter** voice for the `note` form (see `docs/COMPANIONS.md`) — a Substack Note, or any feed
post that announces a piece. Copy it into your instance's `styles/` and tune it.

## What this voice is

The Note is the piece's first sentence to a stranger scrolling past. It is plain text: no markdown,
no links (the tool adds the post's own link, which the feed turns into a card). The piece's own best
lines, lifted verbatim, are usually right.

## Do

- One to three short paragraphs.
- Lead with the concrete thing — the scene, the claim, the surprise — not with *new post*.
- Sound like the piece. A note that sounds like marketing is a different author.

## Don't

- Don't summarize the whole argument; give the reader a reason to open it.
- Don't announce (*I wrote about…*, *In my latest post…*).
- No hashtags, no emoji as punctuation, no second link.
